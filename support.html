SMS Validation

SMS validation is the process of verifying your identity by receiving a text message with a verification code. With ReceiveSMS, you can use virtual phone numbers to receive these codes without exposing your personal phone number. Simply purchase a virtual number from your preferred country, use it for the verification you need, and the SMS will appear instantly in your dashboard.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the Profile page in the app. Once you request deletion, your account will be permanently removed within 6 months and your account balance will become inaccessible.

Important: Account deletion is irreversible. Make sure to use any remaining balance before deleting your account.
